# Research roadmap

Each milestone requires reviewable outputs, reproducible checks and a recorded completion decision. Preparatory software checks, registered experiments, empirical findings and formal proofs are distinct outputs.

| Milestone | Scope | Completion criterion |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | Specification and independent baseline | Audited conventions, source records, environment lock and reproduced baseline targets |
| M1 | Mathematical and computational foundations; registration preparation | Tested methods, synthetic workflow, exact draft, exposure declaration, three public repositories and passing hosted checks |
| M2 | H1 registration, validation, analysis and core note | Verified public registration, registered acceptance checks, frozen analysis and reproducible note |
| M3 | Extensions E1-E4 | Registration and validation for each extension, frozen analyses and specified Holm family adjustment |
| M4 | Paper II theory and simulations | Literature review, registered design, retained simulation results and calibrated conclusions |
| M5 | Formal stability results | Reviewed statements, checked proofs, executable examples and axiom audit |
| M6 | Reusable Python library | Validated methods, reproducibility checks, documentation and release criteria |
| M7 | Manuscripts and replication archives | Checked claims, reproducible figures and clean-environment rebuilds |
| M8 | External review and maintenance | Recorded submissions, responses, contribution decisions and replication or errata evidence |

M1 comprises M1a (estimators and root geometry), M1b (rolling calculations and episodes), M1c.1 (surrogate mechanics) and M1c.2 (secondary methods, protocol and infrastructure). Current evidence is linked in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).

## Dependencies

- G0 requires verified public repositories and passing hosted checks for all three components.
- UK observations remain outside the analysis until G1 verifies a public, immutable registration and its identical protocol attachment.
- Small synthetic development fixtures are distinct from official registered validation experiments.
- Paper II requires its own frozen simulation design; its real-data applications follow the Paper I analysis freeze.
- Formal proof work may proceed independently of empirical outcomes, subject to its blueprint and coordination requirements. A compiled setup example does not establish the programme theorem.
- External acceptance and peer review require actual evidence. Null results, failed calibration and declined contributions are reported as such.
