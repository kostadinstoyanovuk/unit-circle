## D-034 - 2026-09-28 - Paper II implementation choices A1-A7; Kufenko (2016) read in full

Adopted before any Paper II cell is registered or run. Where D-031 is silent, the Paper II simulation code settles seven implementation details, listed in its change record, and they are adopted as written. (A1) Under the descriptive t(5) law every draw, the two start draws included, is Student t with 5 degrees of freedom scaled to unit variance and passes through the same Cholesky factor of the stationary covariance; the start therefore has the stationary covariance but not an exact stationary law, which has no closed form under t innovations, and no burn-in is used. (A2) The confirmation re-run of D-031 (11) covers every boundary cell of the selected test's meaning at all six sample sizes, n = 30 included, without the kink (54 cells for C_R, 48 for C_S); the t(5) check covers the boundary cells with n >= 50 only (45 and 40 cells); R = 10,000 and B = 999 in both. (A3) The Monte Carlo standard error in the tie rule of D-031 (9) is the paired standard error, because the candidates share their series; ties are measured against the candidate with the highest averaged power; "differ by less than" is strict, exact equality is also a tie, and an exact tie at the top goes to the earlier candidate in the tie order. (A4) Streams 4 and 6 use sub-stage words: the series come from (cell, 1) and the bootstrap of replication r from (cell, 2, r). (A5) The dense grid of D-031 (12) saves all three estimators; the headline map is least squares. (A6) The regeneration test of D-031 (13) regenerates every 17th saved cell of each kind in sorted order, a deterministic sample that draws no random numbers. For D-031 (14), Kufenko (2016; Hohenheim discussion paper 48/2016, EconStor handle 10419/129729) was read in full on 28 September (37 pages, through an automated text extraction; PDF SHA-256 02d8e031514c65fdea0ee253c33a39dd41c47b211dc0660d26225244d7ccf583). It offers no test of the AR(2) no-cycle nulls and no calibrated error rate, so it does not change the Paper II design; it is cited as prior work on filter-induced periodicity.
