3. Estimators. The programme's least-squares and Yule-Walker estimators (`uc_core.ar`). Eight fits in the fixed order V1, V2 x early, late x least squares, Yule-Walker, each reporting coefficients, intercept, modulus, period and half-life.
4. Bootstrap (S1.4). For fit k, B=4,000 draws from `PCG64(SeedSequence([1927, 1001, k]))`. Each draw resamples that fit's centred conditional residuals with replacement, rebuilds the series from the first two observed values with the fitted coefficients and intercept, and refits by the same estimator. A strictly stable fit is required; nothing is projected. Report 95% percentile bands (NumPy linear interpolation) for the modulus over valid draws and for the period over complex-root draws, with counts of real-root and failed draws. Bands and point differences between versions are reported; no difference test is performed, because none was registered.
5. AT-13. Statsmodels series, 1749-1924, least squares, B=4,000 from stream `(1927, 1013, 0)`. The check passes only if the period band's lower end lies in [9.25, 9.35) and its upper end in [12.15, 12.35); the modulus band's ends lie in [0.715, 0.725) and [0.865, 0.875); all 4,000 draws are complex with no failures; and least squares on one 200,000-value residual-resampled series from stream `(1927, 1013, 1)` recovers both generating coefficients within three asymptotic standard errors (diagonal of Sigma/n). A failure is reported as it is, not retuned.
6. The S1.1 transcription of Yule (1927) is recorded separately with page references. It does not change these fits.

## D-019 - 2026-09-27 - Foundations checks AT-8, AT-11, AT-12 and the local-level model

Fixed before these checks were run. They complete the original C1 foundations "done when" tests for F2, F4 and F5; F6 (Lemma A in Lean) is recorded in the schur-cohn repository. No UK observation is used.

1. AT-8 (F5). The Schur-Cohn recursion of section 14 on complex polynomials with ascending coefficients: stop as unstable when |a0| >= |an| (no tolerance), otherwise replace p by q with zq = conj(an) p - a0 p*, and declare a non-zero constant stable. Compare with `numpy.roots` (stable if every root has modulus below one) on 160,000 polynomials, 20,000 for each degree 1-8, with coefficients whose real and imaginary parts are independent standard normal, drawn from `PCG64(SeedSequence([1927, 2008]))`. Polynomials with any computed root within 1e-7 of the unit circle are skipped and counted. Pass: no mismatch among the rest.
2. AT-11 (F2). The regression form of AR(2) with intercept on the statsmodels sunspot series, 1749-1924: state (c, phi1, phi2), transition identity, zero state noise, observation variance 1, initial state 0 and P0 = 1e8 I, with the Joseph-form covariance update. With zero state noise the filtered mean depends on the observation variance only through its ratio to P0, so unit variance makes P0 the stated diffuse scale. Pass: the final filtered state equals the least-squares intercept and coefficients within 1e-6. The same run with the least-squares residual variance is reported as a sensitivity, not as the test.
3. Local-level model (F2). Maximum likelihood for the Nile series (statsmodels, 1871-1970) by the prediction-error decomposition, with a diffuse initial level (P1 = 1e10, the first observation excluded from the likelihood), optimised over log variances by L-BFGS-B. Pass: both variances within 0.5% of Durbin and Koopman's reported estimates (irregular 15099, level 1469.1). The statsmodels unobserved-components estimate is reported alongside.
4. AT-12 (F4). Spectral radius as the largest eigenvalue modulus. (a) VAR(1) with diagonal (a, b), 100,000 draws uniform on (-2, 2)^2: equals max(|a|, |b|) within 1e-12. (b) AR(2) as a VAR(1) companion matrix, 100,000 draws uniform on (-3, 3) x (-2, 2): equals the companion-root modulus of `uc_core.ar` within 1e-9, skipping and counting draws with |phi1^2 + 4 phi2| < 1e-8. Streams `(1927, 2012, 0)` and `(1927, 2012, 1)`.
