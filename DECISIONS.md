## D-018 - 2026-09-27 - S1 sunspot sources, samples and bootstrap

Fixed before any S1 fit or bootstrap was computed. S1 uses no UK observation.

1. Sources. Version 2: SILSO `SN_y_tot_V2.0.csv`, retrieved 2026-09-27 (server date 2026-09-01), 1700-2025, every row flagged definitive. Version 1: SILSO version-archive `yearssn.dat` (V1.0, frozen 2015), 1700-2014. The year is the integer part of the mid-year stamp. Raw bytes are kept read-only in `data/raw/` under SILSO's CC BY-NC 4.0 licence with attribution; `DATA_MANIFEST.csv` records their hashes. The statsmodels yearly series stays the reference for AT-1-AT-4 and AT-13 only and is not substituted for the SILSO Version 1 file. Their agreement is recorded as a data check.
2. Samples. Yule's 1749-1924 for both versions, and 1925 to the last complete year of each file: 1925-2014 (V1) and 1925-2025 (V2).
3. Estimators. The programme's least-squares and Yule-Walker estimators (`uc_core.ar`). Eight fits in the fixed order V1, V2 x early, late x least squares, Yule-Walker, each reporting coefficients, intercept, modulus, period and half-life.
4. Bootstrap (S1.4). For fit k, B=4,000 draws from `PCG64(SeedSequence([1927, 1001, k]))`. Each draw resamples that fit's centred conditional residuals with replacement, rebuilds the series from the first two observed values with the fitted coefficients and intercept, and refits by the same estimator. A strictly stable fit is required; nothing is projected. Report 95% percentile bands (NumPy linear interpolation) for the modulus over valid draws and for the period over complex-root draws, with counts of real-root and failed draws. Bands and point differences between versions are reported; no difference test is performed, because none was registered.
5. AT-13. Statsmodels series, 1749-1924, least squares, B=4,000 from stream `(1927, 1013, 0)`. The check passes only if the period band's lower end lies in [9.25, 9.35) and its upper end in [12.15, 12.35); the modulus band's ends lie in [0.715, 0.725) and [0.865, 0.875); all 4,000 draws are complex with no failures; and least squares on one 200,000-value residual-resampled series from stream `(1927, 1013, 1)` recovers both generating coefficients within three asymptotic standard errors (diagonal of Sigma/n). A failure is reported as it is, not retuned.
6. The S1.1 transcription of Yule (1927) is recorded separately with page references. It does not change these fits.
