# Independent verification of the E4 X.3 outputs

`tools/verify_e4_x3.py` is a second implementation of the E4 procedure, written from the registered texts
alone, and a verifier of the X.3 size and power outputs. Its sources are `prereg/E4.md` (OSF dpxqf, SHA-256
`df7e2745b9f30dc9d9ed53526214f8d4f464f175f3180fcff3e1a92e4d03c7b9`) sections 5 to 9 and 11 and Annex A,
`prereg/H1.md` sections 4 to 9 and 12, and the readings adopted where E4 is silent (`docs/E4_READINGS.md`,
D-050). It uses Python's standard library and NumPy only and imports nothing from `uc_core`, `uc_ext`,
`uc_e4` or `uc_ext_official`, so that it does not rest on the code it checks.

## What it recomputes

From the stored 259-value input of each replicate record, and from nothing else:

- the five synthetic vintages (positions 0 to r - 1 for r = 49, 99, 149, 199, 249);
- the rolling intercept-inclusive AR(2) at W = 40 over every window of each vintage at once (closed-form
  centred normal equations; nearly collinear windows by the H1 section 4 `lstsq` recipe), the companion-root
  modulus and Delta = M(n_v - 1) - M(n_v - 9); a window that cannot be fitted anywhere in a vintage fails its
  statistic (R-9.2);
- the fitted null of each vintage (all n_v values, n_v - 2 rows, strict stability as H1 section 6, centred
  residuals), compared with the null the record stores (`null_models`: coefficients, intercept, initial values,
  residuals, the mean removed from them, modulus);
- the generator state of each vintage before and after its draws, from `SeedSequence([seed, stream, cell,
  replicate])` with the cells of Annex A (size: cell j; power: cell 10 x kappa index + j);
- every surrogate attempt (one `integers(0, n_v - 2, size=n_v - 2)` call per attempt, regeneration from the
  first two observed values), so every Delta_b, S_b, K, B', p, q and Wilson interval;
- the stored input itself, regenerated by the H1 section 9 generator at its generation coordinates (stream
  5420 cell 0 for size, 5430 cell = kappa index for power);
- the size and power cell summaries, adjacent differences with the 1.96-SE decrease flag and D80, from the
  recomputed p-values, and the registered pass rule with its wording quoted.

It also checks the design (every coordinate once, none outside, 259 values, B attempts, five vintages per
attempt), one code identity, mode and seed in all manifests and records, each input against its SHA-256, the
internal consistency of every record (counts, S_b as the mean of its five Delta_b, p as (1 + K)/(B' + 1)),
and the runner's summaries field by field where they are given. Counts are compared exactly; floats agree
when |a - b| <= 1e-12 + 1e-9 |b|. A surrogate statistic within 1e-9 of the observed S is reported as a near
tie. Exit status 0 means that everything checked agrees.

## Limits

- The H1 section 9 generator fixes the random draws (one `standard_normal(2)` call, then one
  `normal(0, 3.5, size=257)` call) and the formulas, but not the floating-point order of evaluation; a
  regenerated input may differ from the stored one in the last bits, and the check uses the float tolerance.
  The generator states before and after the draws are compared exactly.
- The real-time stage (sections 4, 5 and 7: workbook structure, vintage selection, levels) is not verified
  here; the synthetic vintages are growth series (R-11.1).
- Results of this verifier on a machine other than the registered environment (Python 3.12.14 under the
  lock) are development results. Its integer results do not depend on the platform; its floats agree with
  the stored ones to rounding.
- The records of the E4 runner hold each vintage's fitted null and residuals (`null_models`). A record without them
  is listed as a missing field (`replicate.null_models`), not as a disagreement; the null is then only recomputed.

## How to run it

On the computer that holds the outputs, from the research clone, giving the directory that holds the E4 X.3
part files (`runs/extensions/E4` if the E4 runner keeps the E1 layout) or the part files themselves:

```
python -B tools/verify_e4_x3.py runs/extensions/E4 --all --workers 6 --report <outside the clone>/verify_e4_x3.json
```

`--sample N` regenerates the surrogate attempts of N records drawn with `--sample-seed` (recorded in the
report) and checks everything else for every record; `--partial` checks the records present without
requiring the complete design (for part files while a run is under way); `--summary FILE` adds a runner
summary to compare (files named `x3_size_summary.json` and `x3_power_summary.json` inside a given directory
are found automatically); `--development` verifies a development run (sizes and streams from its manifests;
master seed 1927 and registered stream ids are refused). In a development measurement (Python 3.12.3, one
process) one replicate at B = 1,000 took 0.46 s to recompute; at that rate a full X.3 (1,000 replicates)
takes about eight minutes of one process (arithmetic).

`tools/crosscheck_e4_kernel.py --root <clone>` compares this implementation with `uc_core` and `uc_e4` on
constructed series and development coordinates: observed Delta, fitted nulls, surrogate paths drawn one attempt
at a time, complete comparisons with their p-values, and whole X.3 records of the package passed through the
verifier.

`tests/test_verify_e4_x3.py` holds hand-checkable cases for each kernel, the random-number protocol against
NumPy calls made by hand, and one test for each kind of corrupted record (an altered surrogate statistic, p
or input, a missing or duplicated coordinate, mixed code hashes, a record from another seed, a truncated
attempt list or file).
