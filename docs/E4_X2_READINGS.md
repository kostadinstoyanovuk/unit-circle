# E4 step X.2: readings taken where `prereg/E4.md` is silent

This document lists every reading that the E4 X.2 source stage (`src/uc_ext_official/e4_source.py`, `tools/acquire_e4.py`) takes where the registered text (`prereg/E4.md`, OSF dpxqf, SHA-256 `df7e2745b9f30dc9d9ed53526214f8d4f464f175f3180fcff3e1a92e4d03c7b9`) does not settle an outcome: the quotation, the options, the choice and the reason. Nothing here changes a registered rule; each entry is a proposal for adoption. Where a reading decides whether the stage stops, the reading that stops is preferred to one that lets an unsettled case pass, because a stop at X.2 is settled by an amendment registered before any level is read (section 13). The readings of the table model (R-4.1 to R-4.12 in `docs/E4_READINGS.md`) apply unchanged; R-4.12 assigns to this stage the choice of the sheets that hold the table, the reading of cells, the printing of titles and notes, and the check that the title, cover and notes name no other edition or release date.

Labels: (a) verified by the tests named (`tests/test_e4_source.py`, `tests/test_e4_source_stages.py`), in a development run on constructed pages and workbooks (Python 3.12.3 under the versions of `requirements.lock`); (b) read from a named source at a named place; (c) inferred.

### The release rule (section 4)

**R-X2.1 Where an edition's release date and time come from.** Quotation (section 4, release rule): "Establish the edition's release date from official ONS metadata: the dataset page, the edition page or the release calendar." and "Release times are taken from the ONS release calendar, which gives them in UK local time". Options: (i) the dataset page's release date for every edition it lists; (ii) the dataset page's release date for the edition listed first only, the edition page's release date for its own edition, and calendar records matched to editions by title; (iii) the dataset page's release date for the edition listed first only, the time of day from a release-calendar record of the same date that lists the dataset, and no date from an edition page. Choice: (iii). Several saved copies of the dataset page may be given (for example those of 29 and 30 September 2026); each dates its first-listed edition, and conflicting dates for one edition leave it undated. Reason: the dataset page states one release date, that of its latest release (b: the page of 29 September 2026, Annex C item 1, states 13 August 2026 and lists "Quarter 2 (Apr to June) 2026, first estimate" first). The saved edition page of "Quarter 4 (Oct to Dec) 2017, Month 2" (Annex C item 5) states "Release date: 13 August 2026", the dataset's date and not that edition's own (b), so an edition page does not date its edition; it is parsed and recorded only. A calendar record's title ("GDP quarterly national accounts, UK: January to March 2026", Annex C item 6) is not an edition label, and a rule matching titles to labels would be a guess; the date and the record's list of the data released with it identify the release. Consequence: only editions listed first on a saved copy of the dataset page can be dated; older editions are never eligible, which does not matter while a later edition is (the rule selects the latest). (a) `test_dataset_page_lists_editions_with_files_and_dates`, `test_conflicting_release_dates_for_one_edition_are_not_used`, `test_saved_pages_parse_as_annex_c_states`.

**R-X2.2 Eligibility.** Quotation: "Use the latest edition of the real-time database released strictly before the verified public-registration timestamp of this addendum" and "An edition whose release cannot be established as preceding registration is not eligible; use the latest one that can be." Choice: the timestamp is `public_registration_timestamp_utc` of `audit/E4_REGISTRATION.json` (2026-09-30T06:27:00.909545Z, fixed as in D-017); UK local times are converted with the Europe/London zone. An edition dated without a time is eligible when its UK day ends no later than the registration instant, not eligible when the day starts at or after it, and otherwise (a release on the registration day, 30 September 2026) not eligible, with the missing evidence named: the release-calendar time. A time is stated to the minute, so it stands for the minute [t, t + 1 minute) in UTC: eligible when that minute ends no later than the registration instant, not eligible when it starts at or after it; a minute that contains the instant (7:27am UK time on 30 September 2026) is not eligible, and a time stated to the second is named as missing. A calendar record counts only when its status is "published" (a release that has happened, not a provisional or confirmed date), it states a time, and it links the dataset. Reason: "cannot be established" is read literally; the registration instant lies 0.9 seconds into the minute 07:27 UK time. (a) `test_same_day_release_without_a_time_is_not_eligible_and_the_missing_time_is_named`, `test_registration_instant_is_compared_in_utc_with_the_stated_minute`, `test_calendar_record_must_list_the_dataset_and_be_published`, `test_release_calendar_time_is_converted_from_uk_time_to_utc`.

**R-X2.3 The latest eligible edition.** Choice: the eligible edition with the latest release date. Two eligible editions with the same date, or an edition entry that does not link exactly one file, stop. Reason: "If the edition cannot be obtained or identified, stop before reading any value". (a) `test_an_entry_with_two_files_cannot_be_identified`, `test_without_any_dated_eligible_edition_the_rule_stops`.

**R-X2.4 Whether the saved pages exclude a later eligible edition.** Quotation: "the latest edition ... released strictly before the verified public-registration timestamp". Options: (i) take the latest eligible edition among the pages given; (ii) also require evidence that no later edition was released before the registration. Choice: (ii). The evidence is complete when (a) a saved dataset page lists the selected edition first and announces its next release for a UK day that starts at or after the registration instant, or (b) the edition listed immediately above the selected one on the reference listing (the saved dataset page with the latest release date) is established as released at or after the registration. The listing is read as newest first (b: on the page of 29 September 2026 the first entry is the edition of the page's release date and the last is the oldest, "Quarter 4 (Oct to Dec) 2015, Month 3"). Otherwise the missing evidence is named. Example: the page of 29 September 2026 alone announces its next release for 30 September 2026, the registration day, so a copy of the dataset page after that release, and that release's calendar time, are needed. Reason: an edition released between the selected one and the registration would be the registered edition; without evidence either way the selection would be a guess. (a) `test_29_september_listing_alone_selects_but_names_the_missing_evidence`, `test_variants_of_the_saved_pages_for_30_september`.

**R-X2.5 Writing the edition record.** Choice: `select` needs G4 for E4 and writes `audit/e4_source/edition.json` once, with every edition considered, its evidence, its eligibility and the reason, and every page used (bytes and SHA-256; URL and retrieval time where a request log gives them). It refuses while the evidence is incomplete (R-X2.4) unless the operator states `--accept-missing-evidence`, which is recorded (`accepted_missing_evidence`). Reason: whether the missing evidence can still be obtained (for example the release-calendar record of 30 September 2026) is a fact on the operator's side; its absence is recorded as a choice, not assumed. Alternative not adopted: selecting without the statement. (a) `test_select_refuses_incomplete_evidence_unless_it_is_recorded_as_unobtainable`, `test_select_records_every_edition_and_refuses_a_second_selection`.

**R-X2.6 Pages the listing step may request.** Quotation (Annex C): the script of 29 September "refused any address not on a fixed list ... and every address containing `/timeseries/`, `/bulletins/`, `/articles/`, `/adhocs/`, `/generator`, `/data/` or `file?uri=`". Choice: `https://www.ons.gov.uk` only: the two dataset landing pages (real-time database, revision triangles), one-segment edition pages of the real-time database, and one-segment release-calendar records (`/releases/<name>`). Refused before any request: a query or fragment, `file?uri=`, `/file`, `/data/`, the other substrings and path segments of the 29 September script (`data`, `previous`, `file`, `download`, `datalist`), and data-file endings. A response that is not an HTML page, or is sent as an attachment, is not saved. The workbook is requested only by the `acquire` stage, and only at the selected edition's file URL. (a) `test_listing_may_request_only_landing_edition_and_calendar_pages`, `test_fetch_refuses_before_any_request_and_saves_only_html`, `test_fetch_through_the_tool_uses_only_allowed_addresses`.

### The structure mapping (section 4, steps 1 to 5)

**R-X2.7 Identifying the vintage-by-quarter table.** Quotation (step 1): "The table used is the vintage-by-quarter table of ABMI levels that these identify, with vintages as columns and reference quarters as rows. ... If there is no such table, more than one, or parts that cannot be joined under that condition, stop and amend". Choice, found by parsing labels and cell types, never by position:
1. Every sheet is read, visible, hidden or very hidden alike; its state is printed.
2. A label column is a column with at least two text cells that parse as reference quarters (R-4.5). A run of such cells is split where a row with a vintage label to the right of the column lies between two of them.
3. The header row of a run is the nearest row above its first reference-quarter label with at least one vintage label (R-4.4, or a date by R-X2.8) to the right of the label column. A run without one is not a table and is counted as an unheaded label run.
4. The table's columns run from the first to the last non-empty header cell to the right of the label column. Every non-empty header cell in that span is a vintage label, whether or not it parses (one that does not parse stops at step 3). A column of the span with an empty header cell is a separator when every table row is empty in it, and stops (step 1) when it holds any cell. Any cell of the table's rows beyond the last header cell, or between the label column and the first header cell, stops (step 1).
5. The table's rows run from the first to the last reference-quarter label of the run. A row between them with an empty label is a separator when the table's columns are empty in it, and stops (step 1) otherwise. A row with a label that does not parse is kept and stops at step 3. Footnote markers are not removed: "Feb 2016 (r)" or "1955 Q4 [note 1]" does not parse.
6. Rows between the header row and the first reference quarter may hold text (units, notes), which is printed with the titles; any other cell there in the table's columns stops (step 1).
7. More than one table on one sheet stops (step 1). Tables on several sheets are the parts of section 4 and are joined, or not, by the model (R-4.6 to R-4.8). No table stops (step 1).
8. Content below the last reference quarter (a notes block, any other block) is not part of the table; its text cells are printed as notes.

Reason: the layout of the workbook is not documented (Annex C); content that the rule would otherwise drop or absorb without a label stops. Consequence: a table with quarters as columns, or with year and quarter in two columns, stops as "no table". (a) `test_one_table_below_titles_is_found_and_no_level_is_read`, `test_no_table_two_tables_and_unlabelled_cells_stop_at_step_one`, `test_hidden_sheets_without_a_table_and_blank_separator_columns_are_allowed`, `test_table_split_across_two_sheets_joinable_and_not`, `test_labels_that_do_not_parse_and_repeated_or_disordered_labels_stop`.

**R-X2.8 A date-formatted header cell.** Quotation (step 3): "A vintage label parses if it is a date or states a calendar month and year"; R-4.4 does not accept Excel serial numbers. Choice: a header cell is a date when the workbook itself types it as one: a cell of date type (`t="d"`, ISO text), or a numeric cell whose cell format (`xl/styles.xml`, `cellXfs`) uses a built-in date or time format (ids 14 to 22, 27 to 36, 45 to 47, 50 to 58) or a custom format whose code, after quoted text, escaped characters, bracketed sections and exponents are removed, contains d, m, y, h or s. Its serial number is converted in the workbook's date system (1900, where serial 60 is refused, or 1904). Any other numeric header cell is a bare number: it does not parse and stops at step 3, and it is printed as "(number, not a date by its format)", never as its value. Only the serials of header-row date cells are read. Reason: the workbook's own number format is the evidence that a number is a date. (a) `test_dates_typed_as_dates_and_as_text_parse_and_bare_numbers_do_not`, `test_number_formats_that_display_dates`, `test_date_cells_are_recognised_only_on_request_and_never_read_without_numbers`.

**R-X2.9 Cells passed to the model.** Quotation (step 2): "the kind of the cell: numeric; empty; marker ...; or other". Choice: the mapping reads cell types and text, never a numeric value. A numeric cell is passed as a numeric stand-in (NaN), so its kind is numeric (R-4.1) and no level is read; a text cell as its text (a number stored as text is other); a logical value as a logical value (other); an error value as an error value (other); a date-formatted or date-typed cell in the table body as a date (other, R-4.1); a blank or absent cell as empty. Merged cells hold content in their top-left cell only, so a vintage label merged across two columns leaves a column unlabelled and stops (R-X2.7, item 4). (a) `test_cell_kinds_in_the_table_include_number_as_text_error_and_logical`.

**R-X2.10 A title, cover or notes naming another edition or release date.** Quotation (section 4): "Its title, cover and notes are printed with the headers at X.2; if they name another edition or release date, stop (§13)."; Annex B step 3: "a title or notes naming another edition". Choice: the texts examined are the document properties, the sheet names, every text cell outside the vintage labels, reference-quarter labels and table body, and the cell comments. An edition is named by the pattern of the dataset's edition labels, "Quarter n (Mon to Mon) yyyy, first estimate | second estimate | third estimate | quarterly national accounts | Month n", compared with the selected label by quarter, months, year and kind. A release date is named by a full date (day, month name and year; yyyy-mm-dd; dd/mm/yyyy) in a text containing release, released, publish, published, publication or issued, and must equal the selected edition's release date; a date introduced by "next release" is another release's date and conflicts only when it is not later than the selected release date. Texts that name neither are printed and not decisive. A conflict is a stop under section 4: "after a stop under §4 the amendment states which file is used" (section 13). Alternatives not adopted: any date in any text (reference periods and base years are not release dates); every "next release" date (it does not identify the workbook as another edition). Limit: an edition named in other words (for example an abbreviation) is printed but not caught by the pattern. (a) `test_titles_naming_another_edition_or_release_date_stop`, `test_titles_naming_the_selected_edition_and_release_date_pass`, `test_title_naming_another_edition_or_release_date_stops_under_the_release_rule`.

**R-X2.11 Order of the checks.** Choice: the title, cover and notes are checked first; then the stops of table identification (step 1); then the model's steps 1 to 5 in its order (R-4.9). Reason: section 13 settles a stop under section 4 ("the amendment states which file is used") differently from a stop at the structure mapping ("the workbook already acquired is kept and used"); the first concerns which file is analysed. (c)

**R-X2.12 Printing and numbers.** Quotation (step 1): "the text cells of each sheet's title and notes (with decimal numbers, percentages, currency amounts, thousands-separated numbers and numbers of five or more digits blanked, as in Annex C)". Choice: the blanking function of the 29 September script (`audit/e4_source/guarded_fetch.py`, `redact`) is ported unchanged and applied to every title, note, comment, document property and label printed or recorded; labels such as "Jan 2016" or "1955 Q1" are unchanged by it. Dates are printed as ISO text, other non-text labels by their description. The availability report is the model's (counts; marker texts; other contents only in a stop, with every digit as `#`). (a) `test_numbers_are_blanked_as_in_annex_c`, `test_no_level_appears_in_any_printed_or_recorded_output`.

**R-X2.13 Records, attempts and reruns.** Quotation (section 13): "A stop under §4, or at the X.2 structure mapping, which reads headers and availability only, is not a deviation: it is settled by an amendment registered before any level is read." Choice: every attempt writes `audit/e4_source/structure-attempt-<k>.json` and `.txt` once and a line in `structure-attempts.jsonl`; a completed mapping also writes `structure-mapping.json` and `.txt` once and adds the coverage of step 5 (earliest and latest vintage, earliest reference quarter) to the manifest row. After a stopped attempt, the mapping runs again only with a committed amendment record named (`--amendment`), which is cited in the attempt. The stage needs G4, the committed acquisition record and a workbook that is present, untracked, read-only and identical to its record; it does not need the X.3 record. Reason: E1's precedent (`selection-attempts.jsonl`, write once), and a rerun must point to the amendment that settles the stop. (a) `test_a_stop_is_recorded_exits_non_zero_and_a_rerun_needs_a_committed_amendment`, `test_map_gates_hash_read_only_and_untracked`, `test_completed_mapping_is_recorded_once_with_coverage_in_the_manifest`.

**R-X2.14 Levels for the registered run.** Choice: `read_level_tables` re-reads exactly the cells that the committed mapping records (sheet, header row, label column, columns and rows), now with numeric values, builds the tables with the model, and refuses unless the re-read availability equals the recorded one (SHA-256 of the kinds). It needs G4, the committed X.3 record with passed checks, the frozen code (`gates.check_code_frozen` for E4), the committed edition, acquisition and mapping records, and the workbook's hash, read-only state and untracked state. It returns the level table held apart and a summary of counts and hashes; nothing in the stage prints a level. (a) `test_level_tables_are_closed_without_x3_and_frozen_code`, `test_level_tables_hold_the_numbers_apart_and_report_only_counts_and_hashes`.
