# Independent verification of the E2 X.3 outputs

`tools/verify_e2_x3.py` is a second implementation of the E2 procedure, written from the registered texts
alone, and a verifier of the X.3 size and power outputs. Its sources are `prereg/E2.md` (OSF 4ncz2, SHA-256
`7b103bb2cc314977cb30446a924d8d975850369f01ebc4819eb6da24f1e08786`) sections 4 to 11 and Annex A,
`prereg/H1.md` (SHA-256 `be447b725317c3b857eb1888dbcedd127d057cd5e0e586a0b2de87e24b182ba4`) sections 4 to 9,
which E2 incorporates ("Every H1 rule applies unchanged unless this addendum states a replacement"), and the
readings adopted where E2 is silent (`docs/E2_READINGS.md`, D-060), each treated as part of the specification.
It uses Python's standard library and NumPy only and imports nothing from `uc_core`, `uc_ext`, `uc_e2` or
`uc_ext_official`, so that it does not rest on the code it checks.

## What it recomputes

From the stored 195 x 2 input of each replicate record, and from nothing else:

- the rolling intercept-inclusive VAR(2) of every window at W = 40 for the series at once (the lag columns of
  each window centred and divided by their root-mean-square magnitude, the closed-form normal equations of the
  scaled columns, the intercepts from the means; a window whose lag block is nearly collinear is fitted by the
  section 6 `lstsq(rcond=None)` recipe itself, whose rank decision then applies), the spectral radius M(t) of the
  4 x 4 companion matrix, and the statistic: the merged episodes of g (runs of two or more quarters with g < 0,
  merged when onset minus previous end is at most 8, the first onset kept), Delta = M(r - 1) - M(r - 9) at each
  eligible onset (r at least 48) and S as their mean; a window that cannot be fitted anywhere in a series fails
  that series' statistic (R16);
- the window fits that the record retains (R9): the intercept, A1, A2 and M of each of the 156 windows;
- the fitted null (all 195 observations, 193 regression rows, strictly stable as R4 reads section 9, the residual
  vectors centred column by column), compared with the null the record stores (coefficients, intercept, initial
  vectors, residuals, the mean removed from them, modulus);
- the generator states before and after the analysis draws, from `SeedSequence([seed, stream, cell, replicate])`
  with the streams of Annex A (size: 5220 for generation and 5221 for the surrogates, cell 0; power: 5230 and
  5231, the cell being the kappa index);
- every surrogate attempt (one `integers(0, 193, size=193)` call per attempt, joint resampling of the residual
  vectors, regeneration from the first two observed vectors; the episodes of each surrogate g in the size mode,
  the observed eligible onsets in the power mode; a draw without an eligible episode is dropped and counted, a
  failure invalidates p), so every Delta_b, S_b, K, B', p, q and Wilson interval;
- the stored input itself, regenerated by the section 11 generator at its generation coordinates (the VAR with
  A1 = 0.3 I, A2 = 0.1 I, mean (2.5, 0), innovation correlation -0.5, the stationary start through the Cholesky
  factor of [[1, 1/3], [1/3, 1]] kron (100/88) Sigma, the persistence kappa * A1 and kappa^2 * A2 planted at
  positions r - 8, ..., r - 1 before the imposed onsets 77 and 148);
- the size and power cell summaries, the adjacent differences with the 1.96-SE decrease flag (decided in exact
  rationals), D80 by the first raw crossing (it may be undefined) and the registered pass rules, with their
  wording quoted;
- the prerequisite record the parts name: AT-12 recomputed through the spectral-radius function (the draws and
  the skip rule of `uc_core.linalg.at12`, written out again here) and the reduction to H1's AR(2) on the fixture
  series, which is regenerated from its generation coordinates.

It also checks the design (every coordinate once, none outside, 195 x 2 values, B attempts), one code
identity, mode, seed, gate and prerequisite in all manifests and records, each input against its SHA-256, the
internal consistency of every record (counts, S_b as the mean of its Delta_b, K from the stored statistics,
p as (1 + K)/(B' + 1)), and the runner's summaries field by field where they are given. Counts are compared
exactly; floats agree when |a - b| <= 1e-12 + 1e-9 |b|. A surrogate statistic within 1e-9 of the observed S is
reported as a near tie, not silently classified. Exit status 0 means that everything checked agrees; the
registered pass or fail of the cells is reported and is not encoded in the exit status.

## Limits

- The section 11 text fixes the random draws and the formulas, but not the floating-point order of evaluation;
  a regenerated input may in principle differ from the stored one in the last bits, and the check uses the float
  tolerance. The generator states before and after the draws are compared exactly.
- The AT-12 draws are those of `uc_core.linalg.at12` (seed, stream 2012 and word 0 or 1), which Annex B names
  and does not print; they are reimplemented here from that rule. F4 and the unit tests the prerequisite record
  cites are taken from the research record and are not run again here.
- The data stages (X.2 and X.4) are not verified here.
- Results of this verifier on a machine other than the registered environment (Python 3.12.14 under the lock)
  are development results. Its integer results do not depend on the platform; its floats agree with the
  stored ones to rounding.
- The records of the E2 runner hold each series' fitted null and window fits. A record without them is listed as
  a missing field (`replicate.comparison.null_model`, `replicate.window_fits`), not as a disagreement; the null
  and the fits are then only recomputed.

## How to run it

On the computer that holds the outputs, from the research clone, giving the directory that holds the E2 X.3
part files (`runs/extensions/E2`) or the part files themselves:

```
python -B tools/verify_e2_x3.py runs/extensions/E2 --all --workers 12 --report <outside the clone>/verify_e2_x3.json
```

The prerequisite record (`x3_prerequisite.jsonl`) and the runner's summaries (`x3_size_summary.json`,
`x3_power_summary.json`) inside a given directory are found automatically; a registered verification of a
directory needs the prerequisite record unless `--partial` is given. `--sample N` regenerates the surrogate
attempts of N records drawn with `--sample-seed` (recorded in the report) and checks everything else for every
record; `--partial` checks the records present without requiring the complete design (for part files while a
run is under way); `--summary FILE` and `--prerequisite FILE` name those files explicitly; `--development`
verifies a development run (sizes and streams from its manifests; master seed 1927 and registered stream ids
are refused). At the registered size (1,000 records, B = 1,000) a run with 12 worker processes took four
minutes on an eight-core computer.

`tools/crosscheck_e2_kernel.py --root <clone>` compares this implementation with `uc_core` and `uc_e2` on
constructed series and development coordinates: the window fits and M(t) paths at W = 32, 40 and 48, the
episodes and the statistic, fitted nulls, surrogate paths drawn one attempt at a time, complete comparisons with
their p-values and generator states, the AT-12 and reduction computations, and whole X.3 records of the package
passed through the verifier; a constant, an explosive and a non-finite series must fail in both.

`tests/test_verify_e2_x3.py` holds hand-checkable cases for each kernel, the section 6 fit and the section 11
generator written out again from the text, the random-number protocol against NumPy calls and a scalar
recursion made by hand, a development run of the research runner checked against its records, prerequisite and
summaries, and one test for each kind of corrupted record (an altered surrogate statistic, p, fitted null,
window fit or input, a missing or duplicated coordinate, mixed code hashes, a record from another seed,
altered generator states, a truncated attempt list or file).
