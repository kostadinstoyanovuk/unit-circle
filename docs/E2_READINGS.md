# E2 analysis code: readings taken where the E2 addendum is silent

This document lists every reading that the E2 analysis code (`src/uc_e2`) takes where the E2 addendum does not settle an outcome: the quotation, the choice, the reason and the test. Sections are cited as numbered in the addendum text (sections 4 to 13, Annexes A and B). E2 was registered on 2 October 2026 (OSF 4ncz2; `prereg/E2.md`, SHA-256 7b103bb2cc314977cb30446a924d8d975850369f01ebc4819eb6da24f1e08786). Each reading was checked against the registered text before the X.3 checks start, because every file of `src/uc_e2` is part of E2's code identity, and the owner adopted all sixteen as they stand on 2 October 2026 (DECISIONS.md D-060).

Labels: (a) verified by running the test named; (b) read from a named source at a named place; (c) inferred.

### Readings of the first E2 code (R1 to R9)

**R1 Power onsets from a record.** Quotation (section 11): "The imposed onsets are the two §7 onset positions, 77 and 148". Choice: registered runs read the W = 40 eligible onsets from `audit/E2_ONSETS.json`, written once at X.1 by `tools/e2_onsets.py` by the section 7 rule from the H1-registered file; development runs use (49, 99, 149) unless onsets are supplied. Reason: the list follows from a rule applied to a registered file; reading it from a record keeps the onsets out of the code. Tests: `test_imposed_onsets_come_from_the_record_under_the_registered_seed`, `test_onset_record_round_trip_and_refusals`, `test_onsets_tool_writes_the_section_7_record_once`. (a)

**R2 X.3 prerequisites.** Quotation (Annex B, step 3): "AT-12 through the E2 spectral-radius function; a unit test that the VAR code, restricted to an AR(2) in companion form, reproduces H1's rolling M(t) to 1e−10 on a synthetic series". Choice: a recorded prerequisite step: AT-12 through `uc_e2.var.spectral_radius` with AT-12's own draws (stream 2012, 100,000 draws in registered mode, 2,000 in development) and tolerances; the reduction at W = 32, 40 and 48 on the H1-design series of stream 5220, cell 1, replicate 0, a coordinate the size check does not use. Tests: `test_at12_through_the_e2_function_is_at12_number_for_number`, `test_development_prerequisite_passes_and_names_its_parts`. (a)

**R3 Structural eligibility in the onsets table.** Quotation (section 7): "Eligibility here is structural. At X.4, if M(r−1) or M(r−9) is non-finite at either onset, the comparison fails". Choice: the X.1 table marks an onset eligible when r ≥ W + 8; finiteness is judged at X.4, where a non-finite modulus fails the comparison. Test: `test_section7_structural_eligibility_at_onsets_47_48_55_and_56`. (a)

**R4 Strict stability.** Quotation (section 9): "a finite companion spectral radius < 1". Choice: exactly that, from `numpy.linalg.eigvals`, with no second numerical check and no projection. Test: `test_one_variable_null_and_draws_are_h1s`. (a)

**R5 H1's Delta in the descriptive table.** Quotation (section 10): "H1's AR(2) Δr at W = 40, from the `h1-frozen` record". Choice: `analyze(h1_frozen_deltas=...)` takes these values from the X.4 tool and records them beside the AR(2) Delta on g recomputed from the E2 values, with whether the two agree exactly. Test: `test_analyze_assembles_every_registered_output_on_a_synthetic_series`. (a)

**R6 Cholesky factors.** Quotation (section 11): "C is the lower Cholesky factor of that covariance (as `numpy.linalg.cholesky` returns it)" and "`z @ L.T` for the whole array; `z @ L` gives a different covariance". Choice: Sigma's factor is the printed literal, which equals `numpy.linalg.cholesky(Sigma)`; the 4 x 4 factor is `numpy.linalg.cholesky` of the stated covariance; innovations are `standard_normal((193, 2)) @ L.T`. Tests: `test_design_parameters_and_stationary_start`, `test_design_series_draws_exactly_the_stated_calls`. (a)

**R7 Planted intercept at kappa = 1.** Quotation (section 11): "replace ... c by `(I2 − kappa·A1 − kappa²·A2)·mu`". Choice: at kappa = 1 the planted segments use the base intercept (1.5, 0) exactly, so a series with onsets at kappa = 1 equals the series without onsets bit for bit, as `uc_core.validation_design.h1_design_series` does. Test: `test_design_series_draws_exactly_the_stated_calls`. (a)

**R8 Observed onsets at X.4.** Quotation (section 7): "The observed eligible set is therefore exactly the two onsets above." Choice: under the registered seed `analyze` requires the section 7 onsets and records whether the observed W = 40 eligible onsets equal them (`onset_check`). Test: `test_analyze_assembles_every_registered_output_on_a_synthetic_series`. (a)

**R9 Retention in X.3.** Quotation (Annex B): "the fitted coefficient matrices of every window and of the null; the 193 × 2 residual matrix; each surrogate statistic and status". Choice: every X.3 record keeps the input, the null with its residual matrix, every attempt's status and S_b, and the W = 40 window fits of its base series; `analyze` keeps the window fits at W = 32, 40 and 48 and the null. Test: `test_size_and_power_records_are_complete_and_regenerable`. (a)

### Readings added with the package

**R10 Development coordinates.** Quotation (Annex A): "All streams use `SeedSequence([1927, stream_id, cell, replicate])` with PCG64." The text is silent on development runs. Choice: a development run uses seed 20260930 and the registered ids shifted by 4000 (9200 to 9231), and stream 9012 in place of AT-12's stream 2012; the registered seed is used only with registered ids and only with the caller's gate flag, and a registered id only under the registered seed (`uc_e2.streams`). Reason: no development number is ever drawn from a registered coordinate; the fixed offset keeps the mapping visible. Tests: `tests/test_e2_streams.py`. (a)

**R11 The quarterly-row grammar.** Quotation (section 4): "A **quarterly row** is a row whose label, after removing spaces at either end, is four digits, one space, the letter Q and one digit from 1 to 4; rows with any other label ... are not quarterly rows and are ignored." Choice: only the space character is removed at either end; the remaining label must match the whole pattern with ASCII digits (a tab, a newline, a lower-case q, two spaces or a footnote mark make it another row, which is ignored). The rule is applied to the unemployment rows; the GDP labels come from the H1 reader and must all be quarterly labels. Reason: literal; a quarter whose label is not recognised is missing and stops the analysis if it lies in the span. Tests: `test_section4_quarterly_annual_and_monthly_rows_only_quarterly_rows_are_used`, `test_section4_label_with_spaces_at_either_end_is_a_quarterly_row`, `test_section4_other_labels_are_not_quarterly_rows`. (a)

**R12 Present values.** Quotation (section 4): "A value is **present** when its field, after removing spaces at either end, is a number in decimal notation". Choice: turning a field into a number is the X.2 tool's step; `variables()` receives numbers, or None for a missing field, and uses a value only if it is a finite real number in [0, 100]; anything else stops. Tests: `test_section4_missing_value_stops`, `test_section4_values_of_0_and_100_are_used_and_values_just_outside_stop`. (a)

**R13 The fallback trigger.** Quotation (section 4): "If the file has no quarterly row in 1971Q1–2019Q4, use for each quarter the three-month-average observation ... A file with quarterly rows for only part of 1971Q1–2019Q4 does not trigger this rule: its missing quarters stop the analysis." Choice: `unemployment_rule(labels)` inspects labels only and names the rule ("quarterly_rows" when at least one quarterly row lies in the span, otherwise "three_month_averages"); a file with partial quarterly coverage is "quarterly_rows" and stops in `variables()`. Establishing the period of each three-month average is left to the X.2 tool. Tests: `test_section4_fallback_only_for_a_file_with_no_quarterly_row_in_the_span`, `test_section4_file_with_quarterly_rows_for_part_of_the_span_stops`. (a)

**R14 Registered onsets equal the text.** Quotation (section 11): "The imposed onsets are the **two §7 onset positions, 77 and 148**". Choice: under the registered seed, `imposed_onsets` refuses unless the onsets read from the record are exactly (77, 148); every registered X.3 check and the X.4 run pass through it. Reason: the record and the text must agree before any registered number is drawn. Test: `test_imposed_onsets_come_from_the_record_under_the_registered_seed`. (a)

**R15 F4 and the data-rule tests in the prerequisite record.** Quotation (Annex B, step 3): "F4 (plan p. 7)" and "unit tests of the §4 rules on constructed files ... unit tests of the §7 rules on constructed growth paths". Choice: F4 is recorded as passed in the research record (DECISIONS.md D-019: the foundations checks "complete the original C1 foundations 'done when' tests for F2, F4 and F5"; STATUS.md) and is not re-run; the prerequisite record names `tests/test_e2_data_rules.py`, which is run beside it. The tests of the section 4 rules that need a file (header records, the release-date record, field classification) belong with the X.2 tool. Test: `test_development_prerequisite_passes_and_names_its_parts`. (a) for the record, (b) for D-019.

**R16 Scope of a failed window.** Quotation (section 6): "Later non-finite or unidentified windows fail the affected analysis, as in H1 §4." Choice: as `uc_core.rolling.max_modulus`, a failed window anywhere after the warm-up fails the M(t) path of that analysis, and with it the statistics that need the path; the episode is not removed. Test: `test_window_failures_raise_with_their_endpoint_and_unstable_fits_are_kept`. (a)

The development results cited here were obtained on Python 3.12.3 with the package versions of `requirements.lock`; registered runs use Python 3.12.14 under the lock. The whole suite was run again under Python 3.12.14 and the lock before the code was published (`audit/e2_x3/preflight/test_full_suite.txt`).
