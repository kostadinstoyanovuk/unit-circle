## 1. Title

*Did UK recessions give warning in the joint dynamics of output and unemployment? A registered extension (E2) of a test of critical slowing down, using a rolling VAR(2) of GDP growth and the change in the unemployment rate, 1971–2019.*

## 2. Question and hypothesis

Before UK recession onsets from 1983Q2 to 2019Q4, did the spectral radius M(t) of a rolling VAR(2) in GDP growth and the change in the unemployment rate rise more than the registered fitted constant-parameter VAR(2) surrogate procedure would produce?

**Hypothesis E2 (one-sided).** The mean pre-onset change S (§8) is unusually large relative to the §9 surrogate distribution. The alternative is upper-tailed, and there is one confirmatory comparison, at W = 40. As in H1, its interpretation depends on the specified model, the fitted nuisance parameters and the calibration. A rejection is not evidence that uniquely identifies critical slowing down (H1 §10; research-repo D-007).

## 3. Prior work and difference

1. The registered H1 background (H1 §2, lines 1–5) applies unchanged: Rye and Jackson (2020), *Scientific Reports* 10, 10481, is documented related macroeconomic work using lag-one autocorrelation and variance on historical GDP datasets.
2. E2 differs from H1 only by adding a second variable and replacing the AR(2) root modulus with the VAR(2) companion spectral radius. Onsets are defined from GDP growth, as in H1.
3. The plan's Paper I literature search (plan §11, p. 12) had not been completed at registration. Nothing here claims how E2 relates to work not yet searched. This is not a claim of novelty.
4. The companion spectral radius equals the largest AR root modulus when an AR(2) is written in companion form. That is acceptance test AT-12 (plan p. 21), which passed in the research repository (D-019; STATUS.md). It was not re-run for this addendum.

## 5. Variables

- `g[t] = 400*(ln(Y[t]) - ln(Y[t-1]))` and `du[t] = u[t] - u[t-1]` (percentage points), for t = 1971Q2,…,2019Q4: **195** observations of `X[t] = (g[t], du[t])`.
- Positions run 0,…,194. Position 0 is 1971Q2, position 39 is 1981Q1, position 48 is 1983Q2, position 55 is 1985Q1 and position 194 is 2019Q4. E2 position p is H1 position p + 64.
- The g values equal H1's g values for the same quarters.
- No seasonal adjustment, detrending or outlier treatment is applied.
- The companion eigenvalues, and hence M(t), are invariant to rescaling either variable, so the unit of Δu does not matter.

## 6. Model and indicator

**Estimation.** At each position t ≥ W − 1, fit to the W observations X[t−W+1],…,X[t]:

`X[s] = c + A1 X[s-1] + A2 X[s-2] + e[s]`

- Use multivariate OLS with an intercept, which is equivalent to OLS equation by equation because both equations share the regressors.
- The first two vectors of the window serve only as lags, giving W − 2 = **38** regression rows at W = 40, with 5 regressors per equation.
- Numerics follow H1 §4, generalised to four lag columns:
  - centre each lag column and divide it by its root-mean-square centred magnitude;
  - solve for both responses with NumPy `lstsq(rcond=None)`;
  - undo the scaling;
  - recover the intercepts from the response and lag means.
- Require rank 4 in the lag block and finite coefficients and residuals. A zero scale or failed solve is a failure, not a reason to drop a window.

**Indicator.** M(t) is the spectral radius of the companion matrix `F = [[A1, A2], [I2, 0]]`: the largest modulus of its four eigenvalues from `numpy.linalg.eigvals`.

- Nothing is clipped or projected, and estimates outside the stable region are kept.
- The first indicator is at position 39 (1981Q1) for W = 40, 31 for W = 32 and 47 for W = 48.
- Later non-finite or unidentified windows fail the affected analysis, as in H1 §4.

## 8. Statistic

For each eligible episode with onset position r, `Delta[r] = M(r-1) - M(r-9)`, with `S = mean(Delta)` over the m eligible episodes and `k = count(Delta > 0)`, exactly as H1 §5.

- At the observed data, m = 2, the §7 list.
- For W = 40 the two windows of each Δr cover r−48,…,r−9 and r−40,…,r−1, and lie wholly inside the E2 sample.
- Record every qualifying run, every merged episode and its eligibility, each signed Delta, m, S and k. k is descriptive.

## 9. Null and p-value

As H1 §6, with the AR(2) replaced by the VAR(2).

**Fitted null.**

- Fit the intercept-inclusive OLS VAR(2) once to all **195** observations (**193** regression rows), with the §6 numerics.
- Require **strict stability**: a finite companion spectral radius **< 1**. An unstable or non-finite fit fails the comparison before any draws. There is no projection.
- Form the **193 × 2** matrix of conditional residual vectors and subtract each column's arithmetic mean explicitly. Do not standardise them or apply a degrees-of-freedom correction.

**Surrogates.**

- For each of exactly **B = 1,000 attempted** draws, sample 193 **row** indices with replacement in one `integers(0,193,size=193)` call. Each selected row carries both residual components together (joint resampling).
- Generate positions 2,…,194 recursively from the observed X[0], X[1] with the fitted intercepts and coefficient matrices.
- **Endogenous onsets (primary).** Refit every W = 40 window, compute M, detect the negative runs of the surrogate **g** (the first component), merge them, apply eligibility and compute S_b.
- Drop and count a draw only when it has no eligible episode. Failures are recorded and invalidate the comparison exactly as H1 §6. The identity attempted = retained + no_eligible + failed holds.
- Raw p-value: `p = (1+K)/(B'+1)`, with K = count(S_b ≥ S) counting ties and B′ the number of retained draws. If B′ = 0, p is undefined. Also report B′, K, the grid spacing, q = K/B′ and its 95% Wilson interval, as in H1 §6. Do not restart with a new seed or increase B after seeing the output.

**Decision rule DR-E2.** The E2 comparison rejects when its **Holm-adjusted p ≤ 0.05** (§10). The raw p is reported when E2 is frozen, labelled "raw, not family-adjusted". No rejection is declared from raw p alone.

**Random numbers.** NumPy `Generator(PCG64(SeedSequence([1927, stream_id, cell, replicate])))`, with the stream ids in Annex A. Master seed **1927**, as in H1 §8. All H1 §8 conventions apply: attempts in numerical order, all innovations for an attempt drawn before its recursion, no randomness consumed by fits or onset detection, and generator states recorded before and after each analysis.

## 11. Outcome rule and the X.3 synthetic design

**Outcome (as H1 §10, with the Holm-adjusted p of §10).**

- Adjusted p ≤ 0.05: "The observed mean pre-onset change in the joint dynamics was unusually large relative to the registered fitted constant-VAR(2) surrogate procedure." Report S, its sign, every Delta, k/m, raw and adjusted p and all counts. If S ≤ 0, say so.
- Adjusted p > 0.05: inconclusive. "The E2 comparison did not detect an unusually large pre-onset change under the registered surrogate model." Also report the H1 Branch B diagnostic: adjusted p > 0.05, D80 defined, and the upper end of the 90% episode interval strictly below D80. It is labelled a diagnostic, not a finding of absence. If D80 or the interval is undefined, report the diagnostic as unavailable.
- Undefined p or failure: not estimable or failed, with reasons.

**X.3 synthetic design.** These are the AT-15 and AT-16 checks adapted to E2. They are run after registration and before any MGSX value is read, without tuning the pipeline. None has been run.

**Generating VAR (D-023, E2-4).** Every series has **195** observations, one per E2 position. The parameters are:

`A1 = 0.3·I2`, `A2 = 0.1·I2`, `mu = (2.5, 0)`, `c = (I2 − A1 − A2)·mu = (1.5, 0)`

- Innovations are Gaussian with covariance `Sigma = [[3.5², −0.5·3.5·1], [−0.5·3.5·1, 1²]]`, so the innovation correlation is **−0.5**.
- Each component is H1's AR(2) (roots 0.5 and −0.2), so the companion eigenvalues are {0.5, 0.5, −0.2, −0.2} and M = 0.5, as in H1.
- There is no cross-lag. The only coupling is the innovation correlation, which exercises the joint resampling. Its sign follows the textbook comovement of output and unemployment; its size is a design choice, not an estimate. No UK data were used to set it.
- The unit of Δu is irrelevant (§5).

**Initialisation.** No burn-in. Draw `(g0, du0, g1, du1)` from the stationary distribution, whose covariance in that order is exactly

`[[1, 1/3], [1/3, 1]] ⊗ (100/88)·Sigma`,

because both components share H1's AR(2) polynomial. This equals the discrete Lyapunov solution to within 2e−15 (checked numerically on 29 September 2026; no random numbers drawn).

- Set `(g0, du0, g1, du1) = (2.5, 0, 2.5, 0) + C z`, where C is the lower Cholesky factor of that covariance (as `numpy.linalg.cholesky` returns it) and z comes from one `standard_normal(4)` call.
- Draw the remaining innovations in one `standard_normal((193,2))` call, form each innovation vector as `e = L z`, where z is a row of the array taken as a column vector and L is the lower Cholesky factor of Sigma, `[[3.5, 0], [−0.5, 0.8660254037844386]]` (in NumPy, `z @ L.T` for the whole array; `z @ L` gives a different covariance), and generate positions 2,…,194 recursively in order, row i of the array giving the innovation at position i + 2.
- The initial pair is drawn once for each series and held fixed for all of that series' fitted-null surrogates. The fitted null is estimated from the generated series by §9; it does not use the known generating parameters.

**Size (AT-15 adapted).**

- 200 series from stream 5220 (cell 0, replicates 0–199), with surrogates from stream 5221 (matching replicate).
- Each is analysed in the E2 primary mode: W = 40, onsets from the synthetic g, 1,000 joint residual-vector surrogates.
- Pass: an empirical rejection rate (raw p ≤ 0.05) between **0.02 and 0.09** inclusive, with all 200 replicates valid. Failure accounting and uncertainty reporting follow H1 §9.

**Power (AT-16 adapted; "sized to" the eligible onsets, plan p. 12).**

- κ ∈ {1.0, 1.2, 1.4, 1.6}, **200** series each. Generation uses stream 5230 (cells 0–3 in κ order, replicates 0–199); fixed-onset surrogates use stream 5231 (matching cell and replicate).
- The imposed onsets are the **two §7 onset positions, 77 and 148** (1990Q3 and 2008Q2 in the E2 calendar).
- In the eight positions r−8,…,r−1 before each onset, that is 69–76 and 140–147, replace `A1, A2` by `kappa·A1, kappa²·A2` and c by `(I2 − kappa·A1 − kappa²·A2)·mu`, so that only persistence changes. Everywhere else, including the onsets, use the base values. This scales all four companion eigenvalues by κ, giving a planted M of 0.5, 0.6, 0.7 and 0.8 (checked numerically on 29 September 2026).
- The two planted segments are disjoint and contain no onset. The W = 40 windows used at onset 148 begin at position 100, after the first segment ends.
- Compute S at the two imposed positions with W = 40, and surrogate S at the same positions (the fixed-date proxy of H1 §9). Both positions are structurally eligible.
- Cell reporting, the 1.96-SE decrease flag and **D80** (first raw crossing, linear in κ) follow H1 §9.
- **Pass:** all four cells valid (all 200 replicates with finite S and a valid p) and no adjacent decrease flagged (H1 §9, AT-16). D80 need not be defined.
- **Expected consequence (unverified).** H1's registered power cells, with five planted onsets, reached a rejection rate of 0.235 at κ = 1.6, so H1's D80 is undefined (D-032). The official checks of E1 and E3 reached 0.115 and 0.340 at κ = 1.6, and their D80 is also undefined (D-037, D-038). With two onsets, E2's power may be lower and its D80 undefined. The Branch B diagnostic would then be unavailable. This follows from the eligible onsets and is recorded, not corrected. The size cells are analysed with the episodes each synthetic series has, not a fixed two, so they say little about the calibration of a two-episode statistic.
