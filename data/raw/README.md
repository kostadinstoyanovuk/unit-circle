# Raw data

Files here are downloaded once, never edited and kept read-only. `DATA_MANIFEST.csv` at the repository root records each file's source, retrieval time and SHA-256; `make check-data` recomputes the hashes.

## Sunspot numbers

`SN_y_tot_V2.0.csv` and `yearssn.dat` are yearly mean total sunspot numbers, Versions 2.0 and 1.0. Source: WDC-SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium, Brussels (https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/). They are redistributed unchanged under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). The Version 2 file is revised by SILSO over time; these are the bytes retrieved on 27 September 2026.

## UK observations

No UK series is stored or has been acquired. Acquisition follows the registered H1 protocol after gates G1 and G2.
