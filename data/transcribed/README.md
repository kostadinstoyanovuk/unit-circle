## Yule (1927)

G. U. Yule (1927), On a method of investigating periodicities in disturbed series, with special reference to Wolfer's sunspot numbers, *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London, Series A* 226, 267-298, doi:10.1098/rsta.1927.0007.

- `yule1927.csv`: the sample, equations (15), (31) and (32), the reported periods and the Table IV serial correlations, with page numbers and the printed wording.
- `yule1927_tableA.csv`: Wolfer's sunspot numbers for 1749-1924 from Table A (pp. 297-298).

The transcription was made from the publisher's scanned PDF (32 pages, pp. 267-298; SHA-256 766518d7d4eaa7ab450df444a1299d8c9a7e1a29861c6bf64d8352478cc2993c), retrieved on 27 September 2026. The PDF itself is not redistributed here. The verification is recorded in `audit/YULE1927_TRANSCRIPTION.md`.
